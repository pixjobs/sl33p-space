The problem

Good sleep is hard-won — hardest for people who need it most.

Shift workers, emergency-services staff, light sleepers and insomniacs don't need another static library of sounds and a journal to fill in. They need something that does the thinking: reasons over what worked, acts on a plan, and keeps the night unbroken. That's the job sl33p-space takes off your hands.

The nightly mission

Watch the agent work — then approve.

Every evening, before you say a word, Nova plans and runs a six-step mission grounded entirely in your own data. Nothing is a black box; every step is visible, and the last one is yours.

1

Analyses your history

Aggregates your sessions in MongoDB — recent nights, mood × track matrix, lifestyle-factor correlations.

2

Verifies in the live database MCP

Runs a real query through the MongoDB MCP server to confirm the pick against your actual ratings — the query is shown, not hidden.

3

Chooses tonight's soundscape

Selects the track most likely to work for your mood and persona, citing the numbers behind it.

4

Composes one continuous arc

Stitches settling → transition → deep sleep into a single source that loops at the OS layer — so it never goes silent at 3am.

5

Predicts the outcome

Estimates how you'll rate the night, with a confidence score that reflects how much data it's standing on.

6

Hands you the controls

Approve and start, swap the track, or regenerate. The next morning it reviews the result and adapts.

What's inside

An agent, not a playlist.

Unbroken overnight audio

One continuous, infinitely-looping soundscape. No JavaScript advancing tracks, so a locked phone never goes quiet before morning.

Load-bearing MongoDB MCP

The agent reasons over the live database through the MongoDB MCP server — and shows you the actual query it ran.

Nova, a coach with memory

A consistent companion that remembers what worked across nights and greets you with where you actually are.

Multi-night experiments

Spots a lever in your data — "caffeine costs you a star" — runs a 3-night test, measures it, and reports the verdict.

Soundscapes by Lyria

Unique, AI-generated ambient tracks — an explicit choice you make, never an autonomous money pit.

Insights that build

Trends, factor correlations, streaks and a sleep journal — the substrate the agent reasons over, visualised for you.

Built with
